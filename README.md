One image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026. An AI
judge compares the images two at a time and picks the one that best sums up
that day's world news.

## The day's six stories

1. **Kerch Bridge hit** — Ukraine strike collapses part of the bridge to
   Crimea; Russia hits Kyiv bridges in reply.
2. **Xi, Putin, Trump to meet** — China confirms a historic three-way summit
   at APEC in Shenzhen.
3. **US tightens chip ban** — Washington closes a loophole on AI chip
   exports to China.
4. **Gaza aid plea** — UK, Australia, Canada and Italy urge Israel to open
   crossings as aid groups call the situation catastrophic.
5. **Japan scrambles jets** — Russian aircraft violate Japanese airspace;
   Tokyo protests.
6. **Depardieu pleads guilty** — French film star admits a sexual assault
   charge in Paris.

Sources: Al Jazeera, Reuters, AP — 17 Nov 2026.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` (1024×1024,
634 KB) to `/output/entry.png`. Run it locally with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
